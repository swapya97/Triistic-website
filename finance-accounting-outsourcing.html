What is outsourced bookkeeping and accounting?

Outsourced bookkeeping and accounting means an external team runs your day-to-day finance work, such as recording transactions, paying bills, collecting receivables, closing the month and preparing reports, inside your own accounting system. Triistic Global provides this for US businesses and CPA firms through a dedicated team of Chartered Accountants, with a reviewer checking every entry before it reaches you.

Who it is for

  • Start-ups and VC-backed companies that need investor-ready books without a full finance team.
  • Growing SMEs and multi-entity companies whose bookkeeping has outgrown a part-time resource.
  • CPA and accounting firms that need extra capacity, especially in tax season.
  • International companies with finance subsidiaries that need one accountable team.

Modular services

Take one module or the whole finance back office

Each module runs as a documented process with a dedicated team of Chartered Accountants and a reviewer who signs off before anything reaches you.

MODULE 01

Bookkeeping

Daily transaction recording and reconciliations, inside your own accounting system.

  • Recording and classification of all transactions.
  • Bank and credit card reconciliations.
  • General ledger maintenance and journal postings.
  • Prepayment, accrual and intercompany schedules.
Outcome: clean, reconciled books every month.
MODULE 02

Accounts Payable & Receivable

Procure-to-pay and order-to-cash, run to your approval limits and payment calendar.

  • Invoice capture, coding and two- or three-way matching.
  • Payment runs and vendor statement reconciliation.
  • Sales invoicing and cash application.
  • Collections follow-up and aging analysis.
Outcome: suppliers paid on time and cash collected faster.
MODULE 03

FP&A & Management Reporting

Planning and reporting that give leadership decision-ready numbers.

  • Annual budgets and rolling forecasts.
  • Cash-flow planning.
  • KPI dashboards and monthly management reports.
  • Variance analysis and board reporting.
Outcome: decisions made on current, reliable numbers.
MODULE 04

Controller Services

A finance controller function without a full-time hire.

  • Month-end close calendar and review.
  • Financial statements under IFRS and US GAAP.
  • Internal controls and SOX-aligned documentation.
  • Audit preparation and auditor coordination.
Outcome: a controlled close and audit-ready books.

How outsourced accounting works with Triistic

Four steps from first call to steady state.

STEP 01

Discovery and scope

We review your entities, chart of accounts, systems and monthly volumes, and agree the scope and turnaround times.

STEP 02

Process mapping and access

We document your current process, confirm approval limits and set up named user access in your systems.

STEP 03

Parallel run

Our team works alongside your current setup for an agreed period, so differences are caught before go-live.

STEP 04

Steady state

Daily processing, reconciliations, a monthly close pack and a regular review call with your named reviewer.
