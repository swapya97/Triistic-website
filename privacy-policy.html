Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Triistic Global ("we", "us") collects through triistic.com, why we collect it, how long we keep it and the choices you have. It applies to visitors to this website and to people who contact us through it. It does not cover data we process on behalf of clients under a service agreement, which is governed by that agreement.

Who we are

Triistic Global is a finance and accounting outsourcing and advisory firm with its delivery center at 2nd Floor, Solitare Business Hub, H-2070, opposite Nico Garden Society, Clover Park, Viman Nagar, Pune 411014, India. You can reach us at enquiry@triistic.com.

What we collect

  • Inquiry form data: your name, work email, company, phone number if you give it, country, the service you are interested in and your message.
  • Email and phone contact: whatever you choose to share when you write to or call us.
  • Usage data: if website analytics are enabled, anonymized information about the pages you visit, the device and browser you use and how you reached the site. Our hosting provider also keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and arrange a discovery call.
  • To prepare a proposal and, if you become a client, to deliver our services.
  • To understand how the website is used so we can improve it.
  • To protect the website and our systems against misuse.

For visitors in the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area, our legal basis is your consent when you submit the form, and our legitimate interest in responding to business inquiries and running a secure website. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for automated decision-making.

Who else sees it

  • Form delivery: submissions are transmitted directly and securely by our mail server to our inquiries team.
  • Analytics: if enabled, Google Analytics processes usage data on our behalf with IP anonymization.
  • Hosting: our web host stores the website and its server logs.
  • Professional advisors: where needed to meet a legal obligation.

Some of these providers may process data outside your country. Where that happens we rely on their standard contractual safeguards.

How long we keep it

Inquiry data is kept for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and for no more than 24 months after our last contact unless you become a client. Client data is kept for the term of the engagement plus the period required by law. Analytics data is retained according to the analytics provider's settings, which we keep at the shortest practical period.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to stop using it for a particular purpose, or to provide it to you in a portable format. Email enquiry@triistic.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in the UK or EEA you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Your US state privacy rights

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law, such as California, you may have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, to ask us to correct or delete it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. To make a request, email enquiry@triistic.com. We will verify your request, respond within the time the law requires, and not treat you differently for using your rights.

Cookies

The website itself sets no cookies. If analytics are enabled, Google Analytics sets cookies to distinguish visitors; you can block these in your browser without affecting the site.

Security

The website is served over HTTPS. Inquiry data is received into a company mailbox with access limited to the people who handle inquiries. Client financial data is handled under the security commitments in our service agreements and is never processed through this website.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy: enquiry@triistic.com.
