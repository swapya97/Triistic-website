Supporting processes, run with the same discipline as your finance function

End-to-end business process management delivered by experienced specialists, enabling efficiency, compliance and cost optimization.

Many clients who outsource their finance function to Triistic Global ask the same team to take on the processes that sit next to it: tax compliance, HR and payroll, IT operations and investment administration. We run them the way we run finance: documented procedures, maker-checker review, defined SLAs and a single point of contact who is accountable for the outcome.

Each engagement is scoped separately, so you can add a process when you are ready and keep your core finance operations unchanged.
