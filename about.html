About Triistic Global

A finance outsourcing and advisory firm built by Chartered Accountants

Triistic Global is a Chartered Accountant-led finance and accounting outsourcing firm. We run bookkeeping, accounts payable and receivable, FP&A and controller services for start-ups, SMEs and established organizations in the US, UK, Europe, the Middle East and Australia.

Our focus is helping organizations move from owner-centric models to process-driven structures by standardizing and strengthening internal controls and governance. Where needed, our advisory team adds financial structuring, investment and tax advisory.

Swapan Nag

CMO & CTO

Swapan Nag is a technology entrepreneur, strategic technology advisor and senior executive with more than 40 years of experience in information technology, business intelligence, geospatial technology, software development, engineering and digital transformation. He has served as CEO of NAG, Inc. since 2001 and previously founded and led Engineering Systems, where he directed major technology and engineering initiatives for government, utilities, infrastructure and commercial organizations.

Throughout his career he has advised organizations including NASA, the U.S. Department of State, the U.S. Department of Homeland Security, the Library of Congress, JPL, USGS, the FAA, Google, Motorola, Jacobs Engineering, Caltech, Southern California Gas Company and numerous state, regional and local government agencies.

His expertise covers technology strategy, enterprise software, AI and business intelligence, data analytics, GIS, cloud technologies, product development, strategic planning and complex program execution. He has led projects from business requirements and strategic planning through software development, implementation, quality assurance and deployment.

Swapan holds an MBA in Marketing from the University of California, Riverside, and degrees in Chemical Engineering and Physics from IIT Kharagpur. He brings to the board a unique combination of technology leadership, entrepreneurial experience, enterprise market knowledge and practical execution capability.
