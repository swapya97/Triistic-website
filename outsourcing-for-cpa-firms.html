How does white-label outsourcing work?

Our team works under your firm's name. Your clients deal only with your firm, while our Chartered Accountants work inside your systems and follow your review process.

Who reviews the work?

Every entry is prepared and checked by our Chartered Accountants. Your CPA then does the final review and sign-off, so nothing reaches your client without your approval.

How is client data protected?

We sign an NDA with your firm, every team member signs a confidentiality agreement, and we work inside your systems with role-based access. See our security controls.
