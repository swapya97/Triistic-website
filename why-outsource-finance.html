How the delivery model works

SOX-aligned processes, controls and segregation of duties

Our finance teams work to documented processes aligned with SOX, COSO and the internal control standards that US, UK and international auditors expect.

  • Detailed SOPs and process flowcharts, periodically reviewed and updated.
  • SOX-aligned control matrices mapped to critical finance processes.
  • Maker–checker workflows with clearly defined approval hierarchies.
  • Separate execution, review and approval, with multi-level checks.

Qualified, globally trained finance professionals

Outsourcing gives you Chartered Accountants and other qualified finance professionals who know US and UK audit expectations and have hands-on experience in:

  • IFRS and US GAAP reporting.
  • Global statutory and management reporting.
  • Internal and external audits.
  • SOX and internal control environments.

Audit-ready documentation and a time-zone advantage

Our teams work documentation-first, so files stay audit-ready all year, while working across time zones turns reconciliations and audit requests around overnight.

  • Working papers, control evidence and complete audit trails.
  • Standardized schedules, templates and support files.
  • Faster month-end, quarter-end and year-end closes.
  • Fewer audit queries and less year-end pressure.

Governance, continuity and data security

The model supports both high-growth companies and complex multinational enterprises, with defined oversight and protected client data.

  • Defined SLAs, escalation routes and periodic governance reviews.
  • Reduced key-person dependency and improved continuity.
  • Role-based access, secure infrastructure and confidentiality agreements.
  • Alignment with global data protection and security expectations.
