AI-enabled bookkeeping with human control

Does automation replace your accountants?

No. Automation handles routine capture and matching. A Chartered Accountant reviews every exception and signs off every report before it reaches you.

Do we need to change our accounting software?

No. We work inside your existing platform, such as QuickBooks, NetSuite or Xero, under named logins that you control.

How is accuracy controlled?

Maker-checker review on every entry, reconciliations run through the month and a full audit trail. See our outsourced bookkeeping and accounting services and security controls.
